Privacy and your study material

Files are read in your browser. Extracted notes, selected images and your AI questions are sent to this server and Google's AI service for processing. Do not upload passwords, confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

Google's unpaid API services may use submitted content and responses to improve products, and human reviewers may review them. Billing-enabled Google projects have different data-use terms. Read Google's current terms.

When signed in, your extracted notes and generated materials are stored in the site's remote Turso database until you delete them or your account. Anonymous generation is not saved to an account. New library quizzes are private by default; publishing makes their questions, answers and selected evidence excerpts public.

Administrators can access stored study material to manage the site. Administrative audit records can retain action details and IP addresses. Deleted material may remain in private provider backups until those backups expire.

Your browser stores theme preferences and limited study state. Private browser study schedules are separated by account and cleared on sign-out. Login cookies are HttpOnly. You can revoke sessions, export your data or delete your account in account settings.

When enabled, Stripe handles subscription payments and Google AdSense handles ads under their own privacy terms. This site does not store payment card details.
